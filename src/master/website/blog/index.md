# Blog

The blog is where you share news, tips and stories. Posts are sorted by date, grouped by category and labelled with tags. Visitors browse them at `/blog`.

> **In simple words:** Pages stay still. Blog posts keep coming. Categories are the shelves and tags are the sticky notes.

::: info
Blog Posts, Categories and Tags need the **Blogs** plugin to be installed.
:::

Open `Website → Blog Posts` or `Website → Configurations`.

## How the pieces fit

```text
Category (required) + Tags (optional) → Blog post → Publish → /blog
```

1. Create at least one category first. Every post needs one.
2. Write the post. Add tags if you like.
3. Click **Publish**. The post appears on `/blog`.

## On the public site

`/blog` shows **Our Latest Posts**. A row of pills lets visitors pick **All** or one category. A tag filter narrows the list. Nine posts show per page, newest first.

## Example content

The sample site uses the categories **The Knowledge Vault**, **The Flavor Journal** and **Beyond Borders Travel**. It uses the tags **Learn and Grow**, **Travel With Ease** and **Tech Savy Blogs**.
