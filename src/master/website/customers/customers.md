# Website customers

The **Customers** page lists every contact record, so you can see your website customers next to your other contacts. You can search them, filter them by portal access, and open one to edit its details or addresses. A customer here is the same record you see in Contacts.

> **In simple words:** This is your contact list, opened from the Website app, with a view of who can sign in.

Open `Website → Customers`.

## Overview

<ImagePopup src="/images1/website/cust_list.png" alt="Website → Customers list, Default tab, rows such as Ben Foster and Grace Liu with the Portal Access badge visible, tabs row and Create Contact button" />

- **Button:** **Create Contact**.
- **Tabs:** **Default**, **Individuals**, **Companies**, **Employees**, **Customers**, **Vendors**, **Archived**.
- **Rows:** Each row shows the name, job title, work email and work phone, with labels such as **Customer** or **Employee**.
- **Row actions:** **View**, **Edit**, **Delete**.
- **Per page:** 16, 32, 64 or All.

Click **Customers** to see only contacts marked as customers. Click **Archived** to see deleted contacts.

### Portal Access badge and filter

A **Portal Access** badge shows on rows of contacts that can sign in. To list them, open the filters and choose **Portal Access**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/website/cust_portal_filter.png" alt="Customers list with the filter panel open and Portal Access selected, filtered rows showing the badge" />

::: tip
Use the **Portal Access** filter to find who can sign in, for example before you send an announcement.
:::

## Create a customer

Scenario: add **Ben Foster**, a person who orders from you.

1. Open `Website → Customers`.
2. Click **Create Contact**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/website/cust_create_button.png" alt="Customers list with the Create Contact button highlighted" />

3. In **General**, choose **Individual** under **Account type**. Enter **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and an address.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/website/cust_form_general.png" alt="Create Contact form, General tab filled for Ben Foster: Individual, name, email ben.foster@mail.com, phone, address" />

4. Open **Sales and Purchases** to set the salesperson and payment terms if you need them.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/website/cust_form_sales.png" alt="Create Contact form, Sales and Purchases tab with Responsible and Payment Terms filled" />

5. Click **Create**.

Aureus opens the contact page. The contact shows **Not Granted** under **Customer Portal**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/website/cust_view.png" alt="Contact view page for Ben Foster with the Customer Portal section showing Portal Access: Not Granted" />

### Form tabs in short

- **General:** **Account type**, **Name**, **Company**, **Avatar**, **Tax ID**, **Job Title**, **Phone**, **Mobile**, **Email**, **Website**, **Title**, **Tags** and the address (**Street 1**, **Street 2**, **City**, **ZIP**, **Country**, **State**).
- **Sales and Purchases:** **Responsible**, **Payment Terms**, **Payment Method**, **Company ID**, **Reference**, **Industry**, and the purchase and **Fiscal Position** settings.
- **Invoicing** and **Internal Notes**.

For what each field means, see [Contacts](../../getting-started/contacts/contacts.md).

::: warning
A customer needs an **Email** before you can grant portal access. Add it now to save time later.
:::

## Sub pages

Open a customer to find these pages.

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **View** | Details and the **Customer Portal** section. |
| **Edit** | The same form as **Create Contact**. |
| **Contacts** | Other people who work for this customer. Click **Add Contact** to add one. |
| **Addresses** | Extra addresses. Types are Permanent, Present, Invoice, Delivery and Other. |

<ImagePopup src="/images1/website/cust_addresses.png" alt="Addresses sub page of Grace Liu with one Delivery address in the list" />

## Actions and statuses

- **Create Contact:** Saves a new contact.
- **Edit:** Changes any detail. The change shows in Contacts too.
- **Delete:** Moves the contact to **Archived**.
- **Portal Access** menu: Grants, changes or removes sign-in. See [Portal and sign-in](./portal-and-sign-in.md).
- **Chatter:** Keeps notes and messages on the record.

| Portal Access | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not Granted** | The contact cannot sign in. |
| **Granted** | The contact can sign in to the portal. |
