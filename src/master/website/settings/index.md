# Website settings

Settings hold the details that appear on every page of your public site. Today this group has one page, Manage Contacts. It stores your email, phone number and social media links. The footer of your site shows them.

> **In simple words:** Type your contact details once and every page footer shows them.

Open it from `Website → Settings → Manage Contacts`.

## Pages in this group

| Page | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| [Manage contacts](manage-contacts.md) | Email and phone in the **Contact Us** block, social icons under **Follow Us** |

## What changes on the public site

| Admin field | Public site |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** and **Phone** | Footer block **Contact Us** |
| Social links | Footer icons under **Follow Us** |

::: tip
Set your contact details before you publish anything else. The footer is on every page, so visitors see it first.
:::
