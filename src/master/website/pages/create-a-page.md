# Create a page

A page holds text, links and pictures that describe your business. You give it a title, write the content and choose whether its link shows in the top menu, the footer or both. Search engines read the optional SEO fields to describe the page in their results.

> **In simple words:** Fill in the title and content, pick the menu switches, click **Create**. Then publish the page so visitors can see it.

Open `Website → Pages`.

## Overview

<!-- TODO-SCREENSHOT: /images1/website/page_list.png | Pages list on the Default tab with Home, About Us, Our Services, Contact Us, FAQ rows, tabs and New Page button visible -->

- **Button:** **New Page**.
- **Tabs:** **Default** and **Archived**. Archived pages sit on the second tab.
- **Columns:** **Title**, **Slug**, **Is Published**, **Created At**.
- **Hidden columns:** **Created By**, **Is Visible Header Menu**, **Is Visible Footer Menu**, **Updated At**. Turn them on with the column toggle.
- **Group by:** **Created At**.
- **Row actions:** **View**, **Edit**, **Delete**.

## Create a page

Scenario: add a page called **Our Team** to the top menu and the footer.

1. Open `Website → Pages` and click **New Page**.

<!-- TODO-SCREENSHOT: /images1/website/page_new_button.png | Pages list with the New Page button highlighted -->

### General

2. Fill in the **General** section.

- **_Title:_** Required. The name visitors see, for example `Our Team`.
- **_Slug:_** Filled in from the title as you type, for example `our-team`. After you save, Aureus locks it. This becomes the web address.
- **_Content:_** Required. The body of the page. Use the editor toolbar for headings, bold, lists and links.

<!-- TODO-SCREENSHOT: /images1/website/page_form_general.png | New page form General section filled: Title Our Team, Slug our-team, Content with a heading and two short paragraphs -->

### SEO

3. Open the **SEO** section. These fields help search engines describe your page.

- **_Meta Title:_** A short headline for search results.
- **_Meta Keywords:_** Words that match the page, separated by commas.
- **_Meta Description:_** One or two sentences that summarise the page. Keep it under 160 characters.

<!-- TODO-SCREENSHOT: /images1/website/page_form_seo.png | SEO section filled with a meta title, keywords and a description for Our Team -->

### Settings

4. Open the **Settings** section and choose where the link appears.

- **_Is Visible Header Menu:_** Turn on to show the link in the top bar.
- **_Is Visible Footer Menu:_** Turn on to list the page under **Useful Links** in the footer.

<!-- TODO-SCREENSHOT: /images1/website/page_form_settings.png | Settings section with both Is Visible Header Menu and Is Visible Footer Menu switched on -->

5. Click **Create**. Other buttons: **Create & create another** saves and opens a blank form. **Cancel** leaves without saving.

<!-- TODO-SCREENSHOT: /images1/website/page_created.png | The page view after saving, with the Page created message and Publish button visible -->

Aureus shows **Page created**. The page is a draft for now. See [Publish and draft](publish-and-draft.md).

## The content editor

The **Content** field is a rich text editor. Use it to:

- Add headings to split long text.
- Make words bold or italic.
- Build bullet and numbered lists.
- Add links to other pages or sites.

::: tip
Write short paragraphs. Visitors skim web pages.
:::

## What the visitor sees

After you publish, the page opens at `/pages/our-team`. The title and content fill the main area. If you turned on **Is Visible Header Menu**, the title appears in the top bar next to **Blog**. If you turned on **Is Visible Footer Menu**, it appears under **Useful Links** in the footer.

<!-- TODO-SCREENSHOT: /images1/website/page_public.png | Public page /pages/our-team in a browser with Our Team in the top bar and content shown -->

## Edit a page

Click **Edit** on the row. Change the fields, then click **Save**. Aureus shows **Page updated**. The slug stays locked.
