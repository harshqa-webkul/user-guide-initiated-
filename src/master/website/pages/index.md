# Pages

Pages are the fixed parts of your website. Think of About Us, Our Services, Contact Us or FAQ. Unlike blog posts, they do not change often and they are not sorted by date. Visitors reach them from the top bar, the footer or a direct link.

> **In simple words:** A page is a standing sheet of information. You write it once, choose where its link appears, then publish it.

Open `Website → Pages`.

## How a page reaches the visitor

```text
New Page → Draft → Publish → /pages/{slug} is live → link in header or footer menu
```

1. You fill in the form. The page starts as a **draft**.
2. You click **Publish**. Visitors can now open it.
3. The menu switches decide where the link shows.

::: info
The page with the slug `home` is your home page. Visitors see it at `/`, not at `/pages/home`.
:::

## Pages that already exist

A fresh site has these pages: **Home**, **About Us**, **Privacy Policy**, **Terms & Conditions**, **News & Announcements**, **Our Services**, **Contact Us**, **Careers** and **FAQ**. Edit them instead of starting from nothing.
