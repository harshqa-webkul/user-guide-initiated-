# Overview calendar

The Overview is a year calendar that shows time off requests for everyone. It helps you see who is away and spot busy weeks. You can open, edit and delete a request from it, and create a new one.

> **In simple words:** Open Overview to see the whole year of requests on one page.

Menu: `Time Off → Overview`.

::: info Before you start
Requests must exist. See [Request time off](request-time-off.md).
:::

## What you see

The page is titled **Overview**. The calendar title is the year, for example **2026**. All twelve months show as small month grids, Sunday to Saturday.

- **Toolbar:** arrows, **today**, and the views **month**, **week** and **day**.
- **Bars:** each request is a bar in the colour of its leave type. The bar shows the type, not the employee name.
- **New Time Off:** a button to create a request.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/overview_year.png" alt="Overview year calendar 2026 with coloured bars for Paid Time Off, Unpaid, Sick Time Off and Training Time Off requests" />

Example: Paid Time Off bars cover 10 to 14 August and 24 to 28 August. An Unpaid bar covers 5 to 7 August.

## Open a request

1. Click a bar, for example the Unpaid bar on 5 to 7 August.
2. The **View** window shows **Time Off Type**, **Request Date From**, **Request Date To**, **Duration** (**3 day(s)**), **Description** and **State** (**To Approve**).
3. Use **Edit** to change it, **Delete** to remove it, or **Cancel** to close.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/overview_event_view.png" alt="View window for the Unpaid request 5 to 7 August with State To Approve and Edit, Delete, Cancel buttons" />

## Create a request

Click **New Time Off**. The form is the same as in [Request time off](request-time-off.md).

::: tip
Approving or refusing happens in [Manage requests](manage-requests.md), not here.
:::
