# Overview calendar

The Overview is a year calendar of the time off requests of all employees. It helps you spot busy weeks and the leave types booked. The bars show the leave type, not the person. You can open, edit and delete a request, and create a new one.

> **In simple words:** Open Overview to see a whole year of requests on one page.

Menu: `Time Off → Overview`.

::: info Before you start
Requests must exist. See [Request time off](request-time-off.md).
:::

## What you see

The page is titled **Overview**. The calendar title is the year, for example **2026**. All twelve months show as small month grids, Sunday to Saturday.

- **Toolbar:** arrows, **today**, and the views **month**, **week** and **day**.
- **Bars:** each request is a bar in the colour of its leave type. The bar shows the type, not the employee name.
- **New Time Off:** a button to create a request.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/overview_year.png" alt="Overview year calendar 2026 with month grids from July to December and coloured bars for Unpaid, Compensatory, Paid Time Off, Training and Sick Time Off requests" />

Example: Paid Time Off bars appear in August and October. An Unpaid bar starts on 5 August. Long names are cut short on the bar.

This calendar starts each week on Sunday. The calendar on [My Time](my-time.md) starts on Monday.

## Open a request

1. Click a bar, for example the Unpaid bar on 5 to 7 August.
2. The **View** window shows **Time Off Type**, **Request Date From**, **Request Date To**, **Duration** (`3 day(s)`), **Description** and **State** (**To Approve**). This window says **State** where the lists say **Status**. It means the same.
3. Use **Edit** to change it, **Delete** to remove it, or **Cancel** to close.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/overview_event_view.png" alt="View window for the Unpaid request from Aug 5, 2026 to Aug 7, 2026, Duration 3 day(s), State To Approve, with Edit, Delete and Cancel buttons" />

## Create a request

Click **New Time Off**. The form is the same as in [Request time off](request-time-off.md).

::: tip
Approving or refusing happens in [Manage requests](manage-requests.md), not here.
:::
