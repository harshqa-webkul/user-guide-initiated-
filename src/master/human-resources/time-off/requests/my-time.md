# My Time

My Time is your personal Time Off page. It shows how many days you have left for each leave type, how many requests wait for a decision, and a calendar of your requests. You can also start a new request from here.

> **In simple words:** Look here to see your balance and your booked days, then click a date to ask for time off.

Menu: `Time Off → My Time → Dashboard`. The left sub-menu also has **My Time off** and **My Allocation**.

::: info Before you start
Your user needs an employee record. Create it in [Employees](../../employees/index.md). Balances come from approved [allocations](../allocations/index.md).
:::

## Balance cards

The **Time Off Analysis** block shows one card per leave type that has **Show on Dashboard** turned on. Each card has the type name, your balance and **Valid until** with the end of the current year, for example **Valid until 2026-12-31**. Cards use the colour of the leave type.

Sarah Mitchell has **Paid Time Off 20.0** and **0.0** for the other types.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/mytime_dashboard_cards.png" alt="My Time dashboard cards with Paid Time Off 20.0 valid until 2026-12-31 and the Pending Requests card" />

The last card, **Pending Requests**, counts your requests that are **To Approve**. It carries the line **Time Off Requests**.

Choose which types appear in [Time off types](../setup/time-off-types.md) with **Show on Dashboard**.

## Calendar

Below the cards is your calendar. It shows your own requests, coloured by leave type. A title looks like **Paid Time Off Sarah Mitchell (3d)**. A half day shows **(0.5 day)**.

- **Toolbar:** previous and next arrows, **Today**, and the title, for example **October 2026**.
- **Views:** **Month** (default), **Week** and **List**. The week starts on Monday and weekends are shaded.
- **List view:** shows **No events to display** when the week is empty.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/mytime_calendar_month.png" alt="My Time month calendar with Sarah Mitchell's Paid Time Off request, toolbar, view buttons, Holidays button and New Time Off button" />

### Holidays window

Click the round blue icon at the top right of the calendar. The **Holidays** window opens with:

- **Public Holidays:** names with dates, for example **Gandhi Jayanti** and **Christmas Day**.
- **Mandatory Holidays:** company days such as **Company Anniversary**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/mytime_holidays_window.png" alt="Holidays window listing Public Holidays and Mandatory Holidays" />

Manage these lists in [Public holidays](../setup/public-holidays.md) and [Mandatory days](../setup/mandatory-days.md).

## Create from the calendar

1. Click the green **New Time Off** button, or click a date.
2. A click on a date fills both date fields and shows **Duration** as **1 working day**.
3. Complete the form. See [Request time off](request-time-off.md).

## Open a request

Click an event on the calendar. The **View** window shows the type, status, dates, **Duration** and **Description**. It has three buttons.

- **Edit:** change the request.
- **Delete:** remove it.
- **Cancel:** close the window.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/mytime_event_view.png" alt="View window for Sarah Mitchell's Paid Time Off request with Edit, Delete and Cancel buttons" />

## My Time off list

Open `Time Off → My Time → My Time off`. The page is titled **My Time Offs**. Click **New My Time off** to start a request.

The list shows requests from all employees.

- **Columns:** **Employee**, **Time Off Type**, **Description**, **Date From**, **Date To**, **Duration**, **Status**.
- **Group by:** Employee, Time Off Type, Status, Start Date, End Date.
- **Search** box and a **Delete selected** bulk action.
