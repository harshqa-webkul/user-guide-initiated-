# Get started with Time Off

This section gets you from an empty module to a working leave flow. You check a balance, request days, approve them and watch the balance change. Then you handle a new employee who has no days yet.

> **In simple words:** Start here to try every part of Time Off once, with one worked example.

## What is in this section

- [First steps](first-steps.md): a tutorial with two people, **Sarah Mitchell** and **Priya Sharma**. You see a request, an approval, a denied request, an allocation, a refusal by a manager, a half day and the calendar.

## Before you start

::: info Prerequisite
You need at least one employee linked to your user. The **Related User** field on the employee form makes the link. See [Add an employee](../../employees/employees/add-employee.md). Sample data links **Sarah Mitchell** to the admin user. For Priya, first follow [First steps with Employees](../../employees/get-started/first-steps.md).
:::

## What you will learn

1. Where your balance shows.
2. How to ask for days off.
3. Where a manager approves or refuses.
4. Why a request can be denied before it is saved.
5. How an allocation gives a person days.
6. How a half day works.
7. How the **Overview** calendar shows requests.
