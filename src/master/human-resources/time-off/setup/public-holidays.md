# Public holidays

A public holiday is a date or a short range of dates when the whole company is off, such as New Year's Day. You keep them in one list so everyone can look them up. Staff see the list in the **Holidays** window on **My Time**.

> **In simple words:** Public holidays are the shared days off of your company, kept in one list.

Open `Time Off → Configuration → Public Holidays`.

::: info Prerequisite
The **Calendar** field uses a working schedule. A working schedule lists the days and hours a team works. Pick one that already exists, such as **Flexible 40 hours/week**.
:::

## Overview

The page lists each holiday. The columns are **Name**, **Start Date**, **End Date** and **Calendar**. Use the search box to find a name, for example `Day`. Group the list by **Start Date**, **End Date** or **Company Name**. Each row has **View**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

Sample data includes holidays of 2025, from **New Year's Day** to **Christmas Day**. They use the calendar **Flexible 40 hours/week**. Dates show as `2025-01-01`. Use the page numbers below the table to see more rows.

## Create a public holiday

This example adds **Company Foundation Day**.

1. Open `Time Off → Configuration → Public Holidays`.
2. Click **New Public Holiday**. A window opens.
3. Fill in the form.

- **_Name:_** Required. Type `Company Foundation Day`.
- **_Start Date:_** Required. Pick the first day. Dates show like `Oct 20, 2026`.
- **_End Date:_** Required. Pick the last day. For one day, pick the same date.
- **_Calendar:_** Optional. Choose **Flexible 40 hours/week**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/to_set_holiday_form.png" alt="Create Public Holidays window with Company Foundation Day, one day range and calendar Flexible 40 hours/week" />

4. Click **Create**. The window closes and the holiday joins the list.

::: tip
Click **Create & create another** to add several holidays in a row.
:::

## Where holidays show

Open `Time Off → My Time → Dashboard`. Click the round blue icon button next to **New Time Off**. A **Holidays** panel slides in from the right with two sections: **Public Holidays** and **Mandatory Holidays**. Each public holiday shows its name and its date or date range. Scroll down to see **Mandatory Holidays**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/time-off/to_set_holidays_window.png" alt="Holidays window on My Time with the Public Holidays section listing names and dates" />

::: info
A public holiday does not change the **Duration** of a request. **Duration** skips Saturdays and Sundays only.
:::

## Edit or delete

Click **Edit** to change the name, dates or calendar, then **Save changes**. Click **Delete** to remove a holiday. Click **View** for a read-only window.
