# Recruitment dashboard

The dashboard gives you a quick count of your hiring. It shows how many applications are active, how many came in and how many people were hired. A chart splits all applicants by status. Filters let you narrow the numbers to one job, department or time range.

> **In simple words:** The dashboard shows your hiring numbers at a glance and lets you filter them.

Open the **Recruitment** app from the main menu. The page title is **Dashboard**.

::: info Prerequisite
The numbers come from applicants. Add some first. See [Add an applicant](../applicants/add-applicant.md).
:::

## Filters

The filter bar sits at the top. All filters start empty, except **Status** and **Date Range**.

- **Job Position:** show only applicants for the chosen positions.
- **Departments:** show only applicants whose department matches.
- **Companies:** show only the chosen companies.
- **Stages:** show only applicants in the chosen stages.
- **Status:** choose **All**, **Ongoing**, **Hired**, **Refused** or **Archived**. The default is **All**.
- **Date Range:** the period to count. It starts as the current calendar year.

## Example: filter to one job

1. Open the **Job Position** filter.
2. Choose **Warehouse Supervisor**.
3. Watch the cards and the chart update.
4. Set **Status** to **Hired** to see only hired people for that job.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/dash_filtered.png" alt="Dashboard filtered to the Warehouse Supervisor job position with the cards and chart updated" />

## Job Position Overview cards

Three cards sit under the title **Job Position Overview**.

- **Active Job Applications:** applications that are still open.
- **Total Applications:** all applications in the chosen filters.
- **Hired Candidates:** applicants who reached a hired stage.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/dash_stat_cards.png" alt="Three stat cards: Active Job Applications, Total Applications and Hired Candidates" />

## Applicant Overview chart

The bar chart has one bar for each status: **Ongoing**, **Hired**, **Refused** and **Archived**. A taller bar means more applicants in that status. Use it to see where your applicants stand.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/dash_applicant_chart.png" alt="Applicant Overview bar chart with bars for Ongoing, Hired, Refused and Archived" />

::: tip
The dashboard is for reading. To act on an applicant, open the [Applicant list](../applicants/applicant-list.md).
:::
