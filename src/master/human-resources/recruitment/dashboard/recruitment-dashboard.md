# Recruitment dashboard

The dashboard gives you a quick count of your hiring. It shows active job positions, applications received and people hired. A chart splits applicants by status. A filter bar sits above the cards.

> **In simple words:** The dashboard shows your hiring numbers at a glance.

Open the top menu group **Dashboard** and click **Recruitment**. The page title is **Dashboard**.

::: info Prerequisite
The numbers come from applicants. Add some first. See [Add an applicant](../applicants/add-applicant.md).
:::

## Filters

The filter bar sits at the top. It has six fields. All start empty, except **Status** and **Date Range**.

- **Job Position:** a list of your active positions.
- **Departments:** a list of departments.
- **Companies:** a list of companies.
- **Stages:** a list of recruitment stages.
- **Status:** choose **All**, **Ongoing**, **Hired**, **Refused** or **Archived**. The default is **All**.
- **Date Range:** a period. It starts as the current calendar year.

Aureus remembers your **Status** choice during your session. Set it back to **All** when you want the full picture.

## Job Position Overview cards

Three cards sit under the title **Job Position Overview**. Each card also shows a percentage line with an arrow. It compares the number with the period before.

- **Active Job Applications:** the number of active job positions created in the date range.
- **Total Applications:** the number of applicants created in the date range.
- **Hired Candidates:** the number of applicants who have a closing date, which means they were hired.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/dash_stat_cards.png" alt="Three stat cards: Active Job Applications, Total Applications and Hired Candidates" />

## Applicant Overview chart

The bar chart has one bar for each status: **Ongoing**, **Hired**, **Refused** and **Archived**. A taller bar means more applicants in that status. The legend reads **Applicant Overview**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/dash_applicant_chart.png" alt="Applicant Overview bar chart with bars for Ongoing, Hired, Refused and Archived" />

::: tip
The dashboard is for reading. To act on an applicant, open the [Applicant list](../applicants/applicant-list.md).
:::
