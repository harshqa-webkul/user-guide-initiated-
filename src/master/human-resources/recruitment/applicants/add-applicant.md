# Add an applicant

An applicant records that one person applied for one job. You create it by choosing a candidate. If the person is new to Aureus, you create the candidate on the spot. Aureus then opens the full applicant page so you can add the details.

> **In simple words:** Pick the person, click **Create**, then fill in the rest on the page that opens.

Open `Recruitments → Applications → Applicants` and click **New Applicant**.

::: info Before you start
Create the [job position](../job-positions/job-positions.md) the person applies for. You choose it on the applicant page after you create the record. A candidate is the person. See [Candidates](candidates.md).
:::

## Worked example

Rahul Verma applies for **Warehouse Supervisor**. He is not in Aureus yet, so you create him as a candidate while adding the applicant.

1. Open `Recruitments → Applications → Applicants`. Click **New Applicant**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/applicants_list_new_button.png" alt="Applicants list header with the New Applicant button in the top right corner" />

2. The **Create Applicant** window opens. It has one field, **Candidate**, with a **+** button beside it. Open the list and look for Rahul Verma. He is not there, so click the **+** button.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/applicant_create_modal_empty.png" alt="Create Applicant window with an empty Candidate select, a plus button beside it, and the Create and Cancel buttons" />

3. The candidate form opens in a large window. Fill in:
   - **Full Name:** `Rahul Verma`
   - **Email Address:** `rahul.verma@example.com`
   - **Phone Number:** a phone number.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/applicant_create_inline_candidate.png" alt="Candidate form filled with Rahul Verma, his email address and phone number" />

4. Click **Create** in the candidate form. Back in the **Create Applicant** window, choose **Rahul Verma** in the **Candidate** field.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/applicant_create_candidate_selected.png" alt="Create Applicant window with Rahul Verma selected in the Candidate field" />

5. Click **Create**. Aureus opens the **Edit Applicant** page. Continue with [Process an applicant](process-applicant.md) to choose the job position and the recruiter.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/recruitment/applicant_edit_after_create.png" alt="Edit Applicant page of a newly added applicant in the New stage with the job position set and the other fields empty" />

## Fields

- **_Candidate:_** Required. Choose an existing candidate from the list, or click the **+** button to create one. The candidate form is described in [Candidates](candidates.md).

Everything else lives on the applicant page. Fill in next:

- The [job position](process-applicant.md#details-you-fill-in).
- The recruiter and interviewers.
- Tags, salary, source and medium.

::: tip
Search the candidate list before you create a new one. One person should have one candidate record.
:::
