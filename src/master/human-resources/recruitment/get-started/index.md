# Get started with Recruitment

This group takes you through one full hiring, from an open job position to a new employee. You check the building blocks first. Then you follow one applicant, **Rahul Verma**, through every stage. After this group, the other pages explain each screen in depth.

> **In simple words:** Check the stages and the job, add the applicant, move them forward, then hire.

## What you will do

1. Check the hiring stages.
2. Check the job position **Warehouse Supervisor**.
3. Add Rahul Verma as an applicant and fill in his details.
4. Move him through the stages until he is hired.
5. Create the employee from the applicant.
6. Refuse a second applicant, **Meera Nair**, and then restore the application.
