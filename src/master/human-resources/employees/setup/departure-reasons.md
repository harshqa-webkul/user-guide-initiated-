# Departure reasons

A departure reason records why a person left the company, such as Resigned or Retired. You choose it on the employee when they leave. Keeping a fixed list lets you compare reasons over time.

> **In simple words:** A departure reason is the answer to "why did this person leave?"

Open `Employees → Configurations → Departure Reasons`.

::: info Prerequisite
None. The sample data has three reasons: **Retired**, **Resigned** and **Fired**.
:::

## Overview

The list has one column, **Name**. Drag the handle on a row to change the order. A filter panel and search help you find a reason.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/employees/set_departure_list.png" alt="Departure Reasons list showing Retired, Resigned and Fired" />

## Create a departure reason

This example adds **Contract ended**.

1. Open `Employees → Configurations → Departure Reasons`.
2. Click **New Departure Reason**. A window opens.
3. Fill in the form.

- **_Name:_** Required. Type `Contract ended`.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/employees/set_departure_form.png" alt="Create Departure Reasons window with the name Contract ended" />

4. Click **Create**.

## Where departure reasons are used

On the employee form, open the **Settings** tab and choose a **Departure Reason**. Two required fields then appear: **Departure Date** and **Departure Description**.

You can group the employee list by **Departure Reason**.

::: tip
The plus button next to the field on the employee form also creates a reason.
:::

## Edit or delete

Click **Edit** on a row to rename it. Click **Delete** to remove it.
