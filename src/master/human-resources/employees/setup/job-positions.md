# Job positions

A job position is a role that exists in your company, such as Sales Manager or Warehouse Supervisor. It holds the official title, the department, a description and the requirements. Employees pick a job position on their profile, and Recruitment uses the same roles when you hire.

> **In simple words:** A job position is the official name of a role, with its department and description.

Open `Employees → Configurations → Job Positions`.

::: info Prerequisite
Create the department first, for example **Operations**. See [Departments](../departments/departments.md). Employment types are optional. See [Employment types](employment-types.md).
:::

## Overview

The list has three tabs.

- **Default:** all active positions.
- **My Department:** positions in your own department.
- **Archived:** positions you deleted. Restore them from here.

Columns are **Job Position**, **Department**, **Company** and **Status** (Yes or No). The list holds 14 positions, including **Warehouse Supervisor** once you add it. On the **Default** tab, drag the handle on a row to change the order. Filters cover **Department**, **Employment Type**, **Company** and **Status**.

## Create a job position

This example creates **Warehouse Supervisor** in the Operations department.

1. Open `Employees → Configurations → Job Positions`.
2. Click the **Job Position** button in the page header. A full page opens.
3. Fill in the form.

### Employment Information

- **_Job Position Title:_** Required. Type the official title, `Warehouse Supervisor`. A **?** icon next to the title shows a hint.
- **_Department:_** Choose **Operations**. The plus button creates a department on the spot.
- **_Company:_** Fills with **My Company**. The plus button creates a company.
- **_Job Description:_** Rich text. Type `Leads the warehouse team and keeps stock records accurate.`
- **_Job Requirements:_** A second rich text editor. List the skills and experience you need.

### Right card

- **_Recruitment Target:_** A number. Default `0`. Type `2` for two hires.
- **_Employment Type:_** Choose **Full-Time**.
- **_Status:_** A toggle. Turn it on to make the position active.
- **_No of employee_** and **_Expected employees_:** Read-only. Aureus fills them in.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/employees/set_jobpos_form.png" alt="Create Job Positions page filled for Warehouse Supervisor in the Operations department with Recruitment Target 2 and Status on" />

4. Click **Create**.

::: warning
Turn **Status** on before you rely on the position.
:::

## Edit, archive and restore

Click **Edit** on a row to change a position. Click **Delete** to archive it. It moves to the **Archived** tab. Use **Restore** there to bring it back.

## Where job positions are used

- On the employee form, the **Job Position** field offers these records.
- If you change the company on an employee, the job position clears and you pick again.
- In Recruitment, positions are the roles you hire for. See [Recruitment](../../recruitment/index.md).
