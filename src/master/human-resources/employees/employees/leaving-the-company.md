# Leaving the company

When someone leaves, you record why and when, and you take them out of the active list. You do not need to erase the record. Keeping it preserves history such as skills, resume and notes.

> **In simple words:** Note the reason and date, switch the employee off, and keep the file.

## Record the departure

1. Open the employee and click **Edit**.
2. Open the **Settings** tab.
3. Choose a **Departure Reason**: **Fired**, **Resigned** or **Retired**.
4. Two fields appear. Fill both. They are required.
   - **_Departure Date:_** the last working day.
   - **_Departure Description:_** a short note about the departure.
5. Turn **Active Employee** off.
6. Click **Save changes**.

Example: a test employee called Test Leaver resigns. Choose **Resigned**, pick the last working day as **Departure Date**, and write the note.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/employees/emp_leave_settings.png" alt="Settings tab of the edit form with Departure Reason Resigned, a Departure Date and a Departure Description filled in" />

The reasons come from [Departure reasons](../setup/departure-reasons.md). The plus button next to the field adds a new one.

If you clear **Departure Reason**, the date and description fields hide again.

## Two steps, two results

| Step | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Departure details and **Active Employee** off | The record shows that the person left. The details are saved. |
| **Delete** on the profile | The record moves to the **Archived** tab. Nothing is erased. |

You can do both. Record the departure first, then archive.

## What Active Employee means

**Active Employee** is on for current staff. Turn it off for someone who has left. The profile shows it as **Yes** or **No** under **Employee Settings**.

## Archive instead of deleting

Click **Delete** on the profile. A window asks **Are you sure you would like to do this?** Click the red **Delete** button to confirm. The employee moves to the **Archived** tab of the [Employee list](employee-list.md). The record is kept, not erased.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/employees/emp_leave_confirm.png" alt="Delete window titled with the employee name, asking Are you sure you would like to do this, with Cancel and a red Delete button" />

On the **Archived** tab, each card shows the name, job title, **View**, **Edit** and **Restore**. There is no **Delete** on an archived card.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/employees/emp_leave_archived.png" alt="Archived tab showing the Test Leaver card with the View, Edit and Restore actions" />

::: tip
Archive an employee who has left. The person leaves the **Default** tab but stays on record, and you can restore it.
:::

## Restore

Open the **Archived** tab and click **Restore** on the employee's card.

## Behind the scenes

The departure details are saved on the employee record. Filters in the employee list can use **Departure Date** and **Departure Reason**.
