# Manage users

The **Manage Users** page sets the rules for how people join and sign in. You choose whether users can invite others and reset passwords. You also choose the role and company that new users receive.

> **In simple words:** This page controls how new users are invited and what they get by default.

## Open the page

1. Go to `Settings → Settings`.
2. In the left menu, under **General**, click **Manage Users**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/general-settings/manage_users_page.png" alt="Manage Users page with the four options" />

## Set the options

The page has four options.

- **_Enable User Invitation:_** Turn on to let users invite other users. The **Invite User** button then shows on the Users list.
- **_Enable Reset Password:_** Turn on to let users reset their password. This also shows the **Change Password** action on a user record, and the reset link on the sign-in page.
- **_Default Role:_** Choose the role given to a new user who accepts an invitation. Type to search the list.
- **_Default Company:_** Choose the company given to a new user who accepts an invitation. Type to search the list.

Click **Save changes** to apply your choices.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/general-settings/manage_users_saved.png" alt="Manage Users page after saving, with the success message" />

::: warning
Set **Default Company** before you invite anyone. Without it, **Invite User** shows the message **Default Company Not Set** and sends nothing.
:::

::: tip
Pick a role with limited access as **Default Role**. You can change a user's role later on their record.
:::

## What happens next

When you click **Invite User** on the Users list, you enter an email address. The person receives a message with a link. After they accept, the account uses your default role and company.
