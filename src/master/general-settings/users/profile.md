# Your profile

Your **profile** holds your own account details: your name, photo, email, language and password. Every user can change their own profile. You do not need any special permission.

> **In simple words:** Your profile is the page where you manage your own account.

## Open your profile

1. Click your name in the top bar.
2. Click your name again in the menu that opens.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/general-settings/profile_menu.png" alt="Top bar user menu showing the user's name" />

## Update your details

In **Profile Information**:

- **_Profile Photo:_** Upload a square picture. You can crop it. Allowed types are JPEG, PNG and WebP, up to 2 MB.
- **_Name:_** Your full name.
- **_Email:_** The email address you sign in with. It must be unique.
- **_Preferred Language:_** The language of the interface. The change applies to you only.

Click **Save Changes**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/general-settings/profile_page.png" alt="Profile page with Profile Information, Update Password and Authenticator app sections" />

## Change your password

In **Update Password**:

1. Enter your **Current Password**.
2. Enter a **New Password**. Choose a long password that is hard to guess.
3. Enter it again in **Confirm Password**.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

The new password must be different from the current one.

## Add a second step to sign in

The same page has an **Authenticator app** section that adds a second step to your sign-in. See [Two-factor authentication](./two-factor-authentication.md).
