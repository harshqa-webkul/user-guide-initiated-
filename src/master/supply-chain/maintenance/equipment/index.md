# Equipment

Equipment is any machine, tool or device your company needs to keep working. It can be a hydraulic press on the shop floor, a forklift, an office computer or a server. You can own it, or use it under a contract with a third party. Each equipment record stores what the item is, where it is, who looks after it and when its warranty ends.

> **In simple words:** Equipment is the list of things you take care of. Every repair or check-up is raised against one of them.

## How equipment fits in the module

Equipment connects to the other parts of Maintenance:

- **Category.** Groups similar equipment, for example Packaging Machines. The category also holds a default Responsible person. See [Equipment categories](../setup/equipment-categories.md).
- **Team.** The group of people who look after the equipment, for example Electrical Maintenance Team. See [Maintenance teams](../setup/maintenance-teams.md).
- **Requests.** A request describes a breakdown (corrective) or a planned check (preventive). When you pick the equipment on a request, the category, team and responsible person fill in for you. See [Requests](../requests/index.md).
- **Calendar.** Requests with a scheduled date appear on the [Maintenance calendar](../calendar/maintenance-calendar.md).

::: info
Create the category and the team first. Then add the equipment, so you can pick both on the equipment form.
:::
