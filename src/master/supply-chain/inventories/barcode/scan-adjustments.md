# Scan adjustments

Count stock with a scanner. Scan a location, then scan each product or lot on its shelf. The app builds the counted quantity for you. When you are happy, apply it.

> **In simple words:** Scan the shelf, count the items, then apply.

Open `Barcode → Barcode App → Inventory Adjustments`. The page subtitle is **Count stock by location, product, or lot**. It works on the same data as [Inventory adjustments](../adjustments/inventory-adjustments.md).

## The list

Each card is one stock row. It shows the location (for example `WH/Stock`), the product, the lot if any, and a progress such as **0 / 110 Units** (counted over on hand). A quick button, for example **+110**, sets the count to the on hand quantity. Check and clear icons apply or discard the count.

## Count by scanning

1. Type or scan in **Scan or search by location, product, lot, serial...** and click **Open**.
2. Scan a location. A message says **Scanning location WH/Stock. Scan more products here or scan another location.**
3. Scan each product or lot. Each scan raises its counted quantity.
4. Click **Clear filters** to remove the location filter.

## Edit a count

Click the pencil icon. The **Adjustment details** screen says **Review stock details and update the counted quantity.** It shows **Location**, **Lot/Serial**, **On hand** and **Counted**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/inventory/bc_adjustment_details.png" alt="Adjustment details screen with On hand 110 and Counted 108" />

Use the buttons to set the count to 0, subtract 1, add 1 or fill the remaining quantity. Click **Confirm** to save, or **Discard**. A message says **Inventory count saved.**

## Apply or clear

Click the check icon to apply. A message says **Inventory adjustment applied.** The stock is corrected. Click the clear icon to remove the count. A message says **Inventory count cleared.**
