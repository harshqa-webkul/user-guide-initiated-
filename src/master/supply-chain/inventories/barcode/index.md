# Barcode app

The Barcode app is a full-screen, phone-friendly screen for warehouse work. Workers scan products and documents instead of typing. It handles transfers and stock counts.

> **In simple words:** A simple scanner screen for receiving, delivering and counting.

::: warning Gate
The Barcode plugin must be installed. Without it, the menu does not appear.
:::

## Open it

1. In the admin menu, find the **Barcode** group.
2. Click **Barcode App**. The app opens in a new full-screen layout.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/inventory/bc_dashboard.png" alt="Barcode app dashboard with Operations cards for Receipts, Delivery Orders and Pick" />

The app uses its own sign-in page, **Sign in to Barcode**. Use your normal credentials.

## Screen layout

- **Top bar:** a menu button, the page title and a scan icon for the camera.
- **Sidebar, Navigation:** **Inventory Operations** and **Inventory Adjustments**.
- **Dashboard:** a card for each operation type, with the number of open operations.

Click a card to open its list of transfers.

## What it is for

| Task | Page |
| --- | --- |
| Receive, pick, pack or deliver with scans | [Scan operations](scan-operations.md) |
| Count stock by location, product or lot | [Scan adjustments](scan-adjustments.md) |
