# Reporting

Reports answer two questions: where is my stock right now, and how did it get there? Aureus gives you one page for each question.

> **In simple words:** Product Quantities shows what you have. Moves History shows what happened.

Open them from `Inventory → Reporting`.

| Page | Question it answers | Use it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Locations** (Product Quantities) | How much is where? | Check on hand and reserved stock by location, lot or package |
| **Moves History** (Move Lines) | What moved and when? | Trace a product, lot or document |

## Typical uses

- A customer asks if you have 10 units. Check the stock report.
- A quantity looks wrong. Check Moves History for the last moves of that product.
- A lot is recalled. Filter Moves History by the lot number.
- You want to correct a count. Use [Inventory adjustments](../adjustments/inventory-adjustments.md).

## Tools on both pages

- **Tabs** are saved views such as **Done** or **On Hand**.
- **Group by** collapses rows into groups.
- **Filters** narrow rows by location, category, package and more.
- The column manager shows or hides columns.
