# Purchase agreements

A purchase agreement is a deal saved before you order. Aureus has two kinds: blanket orders and purchase templates. Both live in one list and one form, and you tell them apart with **Agreement Type**.

> **In simple words:** Agreements save you from retyping vendors, products and prices on every RFQ.

Open `Purchase → Orders → Purchase Agreements`. The menu shows only when **Purchase Agreements** is on in `Purchase → Settings → Manage Orders`.

| Kind | Use it when | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blanket Order** | You promise a vendor a total quantity over a period. | [Blanket orders](blanket-orders.md) |
| **Purchase Template** | You reorder the same lines often. | [Purchase templates](purchase-templates.md) |

The list tabs are **Default**, **My Agreements**, **Blanket Orders**, **Purchase Templates**, **Draft**, **Done** and **Archived**.
