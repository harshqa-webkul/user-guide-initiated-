# Reporting overview

Reports turn your journal entries into answers: how much you own, how much you earned, and who owes you money. Aureus has seven reports. They read the posted entries of your company, so a draft invoice does not show up until you confirm it.

> **In simple words:** Confirm your documents, then open a report to see the result. Reports never change your data.

Reports exist in the Accounting app only. Open `Accounting → Reporting`. The Invoices app has no reporting menu.

## The seven reports

The left side of every report page groups them:

- **Statement Reports**
  - [Balance Sheet](./balance-sheet.md): what you own and owe on a date.
  - [Profit & Loss](./profit-and-loss.md): revenue, expenses and net profit in a period.
- **Audit Reports**
  - [General Ledger](./general-ledger.md): every account with its movements.
  - [Trial Balance](./trial-balance.md): the debit and credit totals per account.
- **Partner Reports**
  - [Partner Ledger](./partner-ledger.md): the balance of each customer and vendor.
  - [Aged Receivable and Aged Payable](./aged-receivable-and-payable.md): open amounts by age.

## Shared controls

<ImagePopup src="/images1/finance/reporting_menu_and_filters.png" alt="A report page (Profit & Loss) with the Statement/Audit/Partner Reports list on the left, Date Range and Journals on top, and the Export to Excel and E" />

- **_Date Range:_** Pick a period from the calendar. Quick choices are **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, **This Quarter**, **Last Quarter**, **This Year** and **Last Year**.
- **_Journals:_** Pick one or more journals to limit the report. Leave it empty to include all.
- **_Export to Excel_** and **_Export to PDF:_** Download the report you see. On the partner reports the buttons read **Export Excel** and **Export PDF**.
- **_Expand All_** and **_Collapse All:_** Open or close the detail rows. They are on the General Ledger, Partner Ledger and the aged reports.

The Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, General Ledger, Trial Balance and Partner Ledger share the **Date Range**. The aged reports use **As of** instead.

::: tip
The default range is the current month. If the report looks empty, pick a range that has data. Click the **Date Range** field and choose a ready-made range (**Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, **This Quarter**, **Last Quarter**, **This Year** or **Last Year**), or pick two days on the calendar, then click **Apply**. In the sample data the entries are dated July 2026, so choose **Last Quarter**.
:::

## Good habits

- Confirm all invoices, bills and payments before you read a report.
- Compare the **Trial Balance** totals. Debit and credit must match.
- Use the **Journals** filter to look at sales only or bank only.
- Export a PDF when you need to share the report.
