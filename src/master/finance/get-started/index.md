# Get started with finance

Finance in Aureus ERP is where your sales, purchases and payments turn into accounting records. Before you send your first invoice, a few things must be in place: the accounting set-up for your company, a chart of accounts, journals, default accounts and taxes. This group of pages walks you through each of them and shows the dashboard you will use every day.

> **In simple words:** Do the set-up once, in the right order. After that, every invoice, bill and payment posts to the correct accounts by itself.

## Two apps, one set of screens

Aureus has two apps for finance. The **Invoices** app is the light one. It covers invoices, bills, payments and the basic configuration. The **Accounting** app has all of that plus the Overview dashboard, journal entries, reports, the chart of accounts, journals, fiscal positions and the accounting settings.

The pages in this group are mostly for the **Accounting** app. Each page tells you where a screen exists in both apps and where it exists in only one.

## What is in this group

- [Set up accounting](accounting-setup.md): the one-time step that creates the chart of accounts, journals and defaults for a company.
- [Accounting Overview](accounting-overview.md): the dashboard with one card per journal.
- [Chart of accounts](chart-of-accounts.md): the list of accounts where every amount is recorded.
- [Journals](journals.md): the books that group your entries, such as Customer Invoices and Bank.
- [Default accounts](default-accounts.md): the accounts Aureus uses when you do not choose one.
- [Foreign currencies](multi-currency.md): invoices, bills and payments in a currency other than your own.

## Set up in this order

1. **Set up accounting** for your company. See [Set up accounting](accounting-setup.md).
2. Check the **chart of accounts** and add any account you need. See [Chart of accounts](chart-of-accounts.md).
3. Check your **journals**. Add a Bank journal for each real bank account. See [Journals](journals.md).
4. Review the **default accounts**. See [Default accounts](default-accounts.md).
5. Set up your **taxes**. See [Taxes](../taxes/index.md).
6. If you sell or buy in other currencies, activate them and add a rate. See [Foreign currencies](multi-currency.md).
7. Create your first invoice. See [Customer invoices](../customer-invoices/index.md).

::: tip
Steps 2 to 4 are already done for you when you run **Set up accounting**. Read them as a check, not as work you must repeat.
:::
