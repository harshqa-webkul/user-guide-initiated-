# Invoicing

Invoicing turns a confirmed sales order into a customer invoice. The **To Invoice** menu lists the orders that still need invoicing, and the invoice policy decides when an order is ready for it.

> **In simple words:** Confirm the order, then invoice it from the **To Invoice** menu.

## How an order reaches an invoice

1. Create and confirm a quotation. It becomes a sales order. See [Send and confirm a quotation](../quotations-orders/send-and-confirm-quotation.md).
2. The invoice policy decides when the order is ready to invoice. See [Invoicing policies](invoicing-policies.md).
3. Open the order and click **Create Invoice**. See [Orders to invoice](order-to-invoice.md).
4. The invoice is created for the customer.

## The two lists

The **To Invoice** menu has two pages. Both list sales orders, filtered by their invoice status.

- **Orders To Invoice:** Orders that are ready to be invoiced.
- **Orders To Upsell:** Orders where the invoice status is **Up Selling**.
