# Quotations and orders

This group covers the first half of the sales story: your customers, the offers you send them, the orders that follow, and how you get paid. You prepare a quotation, send it, and confirm it when the customer agrees. The confirmed quotation becomes a sales order. You then deliver it, invoice it and record the payment.

> **In simple words:** A quotation is an offer. When the customer says yes, it becomes an order. When they pay the invoice, the sale is done.

## The flow

Here is one sale from start to finish. Brightway Offices wants 10 office chairs.

1. **Customer.** You add Brightway Offices as a customer. See [Manage customers](./manage-customers.md).
2. **Quotation.** You create a quotation for 10 chairs with prices and a payment term. See [Create a quotation](./create-quotation.md).
3. **Send.** You email the quotation to Brightway. See [Send and confirm a quotation](./send-and-confirm-quotation.md).
4. **Confirm.** Brightway agrees, so you confirm the quotation.
5. **Order.** The quotation is now a sales order. You follow it on [Manage orders](./manage-orders.md).
6. **Delivery.** The warehouse ships the chairs.
7. **Invoice.** You create the invoice from the order. See [Invoicing](../invoicing/index.md).
8. **Payment.** Brightway pays, and you record the payment against the invoice.

## Payment

Payment is the last step of the flow, and it happens in the invoicing part of Aureus, not on the quotation.

- **On the quotation.** You choose a **Payment Term**. It says when the customer must pay, for example "30 Days". The term is copied to the invoice and sets its due date. A customer can carry a default term, see [Manage customers](./manage-customers.md). To set up terms, see [Payment terms](../../finance/customer-invoices/payment-terms.md).
- **On the invoice.** When the customer pays, you record it on the customer invoice. See [Create an invoice](../../finance/customer-invoices/create-invoice.md) and [Register a payment](../../finance/payments/register-payment.md).
- **On the order.** Open the order and click the **Invoices** tab. It lists each invoice with its **Amount Due** and **Payment State**, so you can see at a glance whether the customer has paid.

::: info
Aureus records payments after the invoice exists. The quotation itself does not take a payment.
:::
