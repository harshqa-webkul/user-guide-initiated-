# Product variants

A variant is one version of a product, such as a T-shirt in size M and color red. You do not create variants by hand. You add attributes to a product, and the app creates the variants for you.

> **In simple words:** Pick the attributes and values, and the app builds every combination.

## Settings

Variants are off until you turn them on.

1. Go to `Sales → Settings → Manage Products`.
2. Turn on **Variants** (Allow products to have multiple variants).
3. Click **Save changes**.

## Add attributes to a product

1. Go to `Sales → Products` and open a product.
2. Click **Attributes**.
3. Click **Add Attribute**.
4. In the **Create Product Attribute** window, fill in:

   - **_Attribute:_** Choose an attribute, for example **Size**.
   - **_Values:_** Choose the values that apply, for example **S** and **M**.

5. Click **Create**, or **Create & create another** to add more. Click **Cancel** to close the window.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/product_attributes_add.png" alt="Create Product Attribute window with Attribute and Values" />

You create attributes and their values once, in the configuration. See [Product attributes](../configuration/product-attributes.md).

::: warning
Adding or deleting attributes deletes and recreates the existing variants. You lose any changes you made to those variants.
:::

## Manage variants

Click **Variants** on the product to see its variants. The table shows **Variant Values** for each one.

For each variant you can:

- **View:** Open the variant. From there you can click **Print Labels**.
- **Edit:** Change the variant details.
- **Delete:** Remove the variant. The app shows **Variant deleted**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/product_variants_list.png" alt="Product Variants page with the variant values and row actions" />

In the product list, the **Variants** column shows how many variants a product has.
