# Take an order

An order is one sale at the till. You add products, check the total and take payment. This page follows a retail sale from the first product to the payment screen, using the TechNest Electronics shop.

> **In simple words:** Tap the products, fix the quantities, then click Payment.

Make sure the register is open first. See [Open the register](../sessions/open-register.md).

## Start the order

When the register opens, an empty order is ready. The order panel says "Scan or tap a product to start". The order gets a tracking number, such as **401**.

To start another order at any time, click the **+** tab at the top right. The old order stays open under its own tab.

## Add products

1. Click a category tile, such as **Accessories**, or type in **Search products**.
2. Click the product tile. One unit goes to the order.
3. Click the tile again to add one more unit.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/take_order_add_products.png" alt="Retail terminal with Glide Wireless Mouse quantity 2 and BudsAir Wireless Earbuds on the order panel" />

**Example:** Sofia Rossi wants two Glide Wireless Mouse and one BudsAir Wireless Earbuds. Click **Glide Wireless Mouse** twice and **BudsAir Wireless Earbuds** once. The panel shows:

- Glide Wireless Mouse, 2 x 1,299, line total 2,598
- BudsAir Wireless Earbuds, 1 x 3,999, line total 3,999
- **Total:** 6,597

If a product has variants, a window asks you to pick the attributes. If it needs a lot or serial number, a window asks for the number.

## Change a quantity

1. Click the line in the order panel. It turns blue.
2. Make sure the number pad is in **Qty** mode.
3. Type the new quantity, for example 3.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/take_order_change_qty.png" alt="Selected Glide Wireless Mouse line with the number pad in Qty mode" />

Use **+/-** to change the sign and the backspace key to delete a digit.

## Remove a line

Click the **X** at the right end of the line. The line disappears and the total updates.

## Discount or price on a line

Select the line, then click **%** or **Price** on the number pad.

- **%:** Type the discount percentage. The line then shows "5% discount", for example.
- **Price:** Type a new unit price for this order only.

These modes work only when your register allows them. Line discounts are a setting of the register. See [POS settings](../get-started/pos-settings.md) and [Set up a register](../get-started/set-up-a-register.md).

## Add a customer or a note

Click **Customer** to attach a person to the sale. Click **Note** to write a message on a line. See [Notes and customers](order-notes-and-customers.md).

## Cancel the order

Click **Actions**, then **Cancel order**, then **Confirm**. The lines are lost and this cannot be undone.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/take_order_actions_retail.png" alt="Actions window on a retail order showing only the Cancel order button" />

## Continue to payment

When the order is right, click **Payment**. See [Pay and receipt](pay-and-receipt.md).

## Actions and statuses

- A new order has the status **New** until you validate the payment.
- After payment the status is **Paid**.
- Cancelled orders show **Cancelled**.

::: tip
Keep two or three orders open on separate tabs when customers are waiting. Switch between them with the tabs.
:::

::: info
Prices follow the register's price list and tax setting. See [Pricing and taxes](../products-pricing/pricing-and-taxes.md).
:::
