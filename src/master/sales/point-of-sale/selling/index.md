# Selling at the terminal

The terminal is the till screen your cashiers use all day. It runs in the browser, so any computer, tablet or touch screen can be a register. This group shows the whole selling flow, from the first product on an order to the receipt, a refund or an invoice.

> **In simple words:** Open the register, add products, take payment, hand over the receipt. Everything else is an option on top.

## The selling flow

1. **Open the register.** Confirm the opening cash. See [Open the register](../sessions/open-register.md).
2. **Start an order.** In a shop, pick products. In a restaurant, click a table first. See [Take an order](take-an-order.md) and [Restaurant tables](restaurant-tables.md).
3. **Add details.** Pick a customer, add notes, change quantity or price. See [Notes and customers](order-notes-and-customers.md).
4. **Take payment.** Choose a method, enter the amount and click **Validate**. See [Pay and receipt](pay-and-receipt.md).
5. **After the sale.** Refund a product, or create an invoice for a customer. See [Refunds](refunds.md) and [Invoice a POS order](invoice-a-pos-order.md).
6. **Close the register.** Count the cash at the end of the day. See [Close a session](../sessions/close-session.md).

## Order statuses

An order moves through these statuses.

- **New:** The order is open and not paid.
- **Paid:** The customer paid. The order waits for the session to close.
- **Posted:** The session closed and the sale went to accounting.
- **Invoiced:** A customer invoice was created for the order.
- **Cancelled:** The order was cancelled before payment.
