# Terminal tour

The terminal is where you sell. It has a product area on the left and the current order on the right. This page names every part of the screen, so you know where to click before your first sale.

> **In simple words:** Products on the left, the order on the right, buttons and the number pad underneath.

## Open the terminal

Open `/pos/registers` in your browser, or click **Open Register** or **Continue Selling** on the back office **Dashboard**. Pick your register. The terminal opens on the **Home** page.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/terminal_registers.png" alt="Terminal Registers page listing the two registers with Resume and Open register buttons" />

## The top bar

- **Registers:** Goes back to the list of registers.
- **Home:** The selling screen. In a restaurant this is the floor plan first.
- **Your initials:** Opens the menu with **Orders** (past and open orders, see [Refunds](refunds.md)), **Cash In / Out**, **Install app**, **Toggle theme**, **Back office**, **Close register** and **Log out**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/terminal_user_menu.png" alt="Terminal user menu open with Orders, Cash In / Out, Install app, Toggle theme, Back office, Close register and Log out" />

## The product area

1. Use **Search products** to find a product by name.
2. Click a **category tile**, such as **Audio** or **Accessories**, to show only that category.
3. Click a product tile to add one unit to the order. Click it again to add more.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/terminal_home_retail.png" alt="TechNest retail terminal with search box, category tiles, product grid and the order panel" />

Each tile shows the name, the price and, if it is already on the order, the quantity in the corner. The red **i** icon opens the product information with stock, price, cost and margin.

The blue **+** button next to the search box creates a new product on the spot.

## The order tabs

At the top right of the product area you see the order tabs.

- The chip with a number, such as **401**, is the **tracking number** of the order. It is also printed on the receipt.
- The hamburger icon lists all open orders.
- The **+** icon starts a new order. Orders you set aside stay open until you come back.

## The order panel

The right side shows the current order.

- **Lines:** Product, quantity x price and the line total. Click **X** to remove a line.
- **Subtotal, Taxes, Total:** The running amounts.
- **Customer:** Picks the customer. See [Notes and customers](order-notes-and-customers.md).
- **Note:** Adds a note to the selected line.
- **Actions:** More options for the order, such as **Cancel order**. In a restaurant it also holds **Guests** and **Transfer / Merge**.
- **Payment:** Goes to the payment screen. See [Pay and receipt](pay-and-receipt.md).

## The number pad

The pad changes the selected line. Click a line first, then choose a mode.

- **Qty:** Types the quantity. Enter 3 to make the quantity 3.
- **%:** Types a discount percentage for the line. The mode is active only when line discounts are on.
- **Price:** Types a new unit price for the line. It works only when price changes are allowed.
- **+/-:** Switches the sign of the number.
- **Backspace:** Deletes the last digit.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/terminal_numpad.png" alt="Number pad with the Qty, percent and Price modes, +/- key, backspace key and Payment button" />

## The Orders page

Click **Orders** in the menu to see orders of this register. You can filter by status, search and select an order. Use it for refunds and invoices.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/terminal_orders_page.png" alt="Terminal Orders page with the order list, the All active orders filter and the details panel" />

::: tip
A barcode scanner works anywhere on the till. Scan a product and it goes to the order.
:::
