# Point of Sale

Point of Sale (POS) is the module you use at the till. It lets a cashier ring up a sale, take payment, print a receipt and close the day with a cash count. Every sale feeds your stock and your accounts, so the shop and the back office always agree. Use it for a retail shop, a café or a full restaurant with tables.

> **In simple words:** POS is your cash register. You set it up once, open it each day, sell, and close it at night.

## The two parts of POS

POS has two screens that work together.

- **The admin app.** Open the **Point of Sale** app from the main menu. Here you set up registers, payment methods, floors and prices. You also watch sessions, orders, payments and reports.
- **The cashier terminal.** This is the till screen. It has its own pages: **Registers**, **Home** and **Orders**. Cashiers add products, pick customers, take payments and print receipts here.

You open the terminal from the admin **Dashboard**. Click **Open Register** on a closed register, or **Continue Selling** on an open one.

## The full flow

Follow these stages in order. Each one has its own page.

1. **Set up a register.** Create a register, choose its payment methods, journals, prices and receipt text. See [Set up a register](get-started/set-up-a-register.md).
2. **Open a session.** A session is one shift of selling. Count the cash in the drawer and start. See [Open a register](sessions/open-register.md).
3. **Sell.** Add products to an order, pick a customer, add notes. In a restaurant, start from a table. See [Take an order](selling/take-an-order.md).
4. **Pay and print the receipt.** Choose a payment method, enter the amount and validate. See [Pay and receipt](selling/pay-and-receipt.md).
5. **Refund or invoice.** Return products from a paid order, or create a customer invoice for it. See [Refunds](selling/refunds.md) and [Invoice a POS order](selling/invoice-a-pos-order.md).
6. **Move cash in or out.** Record money put into or taken out of the drawer during the shift. See [Cash in and out](sessions/cash-in-and-out.md).
7. **Close the session.** Count the cash, compare it with the expected balance and post the entries. See [Close a session](sessions/close-session.md).
8. **Check reports.** Review orders, sales details and sessions. See [Reporting](reporting/index.md).

The pages use two sample shops.

- **TechNest Electronics** (short code TNE) is a retail shop. It sells items such as the Glide Wireless Mouse and BudsAir Wireless Earbuds to customers like Sofia Rossi and Tom Baker.
