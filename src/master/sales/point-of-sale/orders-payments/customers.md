# Customers

A POS customer is a contact you attach to an order. With a customer on the order you can send an invoice and see who bought what. The Customers page is a shortcut to the same contact records that the rest of Aureus uses.

> **In simple words:** Customers here are your normal contacts. Add one here or at the till and use it on any order.

## Overview

Go to `Point of Sale → Orders → Customers`.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/customers_list.png" alt="Point of Sale → Orders → Customers list with Sofia Rossi, Deco Addict and Azure Interiors" />

The page has these tabs: **Default**, **Individuals**, **Companies** and **Archived**.

Each row shows the **Name**, **Parent**, **Job Title**, **Work Email** and **Work Phone**, with tags such as **Customer** and **Individual**. Use **Group by** for **Account Type**, **Parent**, **Title**, **Job Title** or **Industry**.

The row actions are **View**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Create a customer

1. Go to `Point of Sale → Orders → Customers`.
2. Click **New Customer**.
3. Fill in the **General** section.

   - **_Account type:_** Choose **Individual** or **Company**.
   - **_Name\:_** Required. Enter the name, for example **Sofia Rossi**.
   - **_Company:_** For an individual, pick the company they work for.
   - **_Tax ID_**, **_Job Title_**, **_Phone_**, **_Mobile_**, **_Email_**, **_Website_**: Fill in what you know.
   - **_Title:_** Choose **Mr** or **Mrs**.
   - **_Tags:_** Add tags to group customers.
   - **_Address:_** **Street 1**, **Street 2**, **City**, **ZIP**, **Country** and **State**.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/customer_form_general.png" alt="New customer form with Account type Individual, Name Sofia Rossi, email sofia.rossi@mail.com and phone 2025550302" />

4. Open the tab **Sales and Purchases** for the **Responsible** salesperson, **Payment Terms**, **Payment Method** and **Fiscal Position**.
5. Open **Invoicing** and **Internal Notes** if you need them.
6. Click **Create**. Click **Create & create another** to add more.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/pos/customer_form_sales_tab.png" alt="Customer form Sales and Purchases tab with Responsible, Payment Terms and Fiscal Position" />

The full list of contact fields is on the [Contacts](../../../getting-started/contacts/contacts.md) page.

## Use a customer on an order

At the till, click **Customer** on the order screen and pick the contact. You can make **Customer Required** a rule in the register setup. See [Order notes and customers](../selling/order-notes-and-customers.md).

## View a customer

Click **View** to open a customer. The page also gives you sub pages for **Contacts**, **Addresses** and **Bank Accounts** of that customer.

::: tip
Search before you create. A duplicate contact splits a customer's history across two records.
:::
