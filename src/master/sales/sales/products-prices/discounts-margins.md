# Discounts and margins

Discounts let you lower the price on a quotation line by a percentage. Margins show how much profit an order makes. Both are off until you turn them on.

> **In simple words:** Turn on **Discount** to give a percentage off. Turn on **Margins** to see your profit.

## Settings

1. Go to `Sales → Settings → Manage Pricing`.
2. Turn on the options you need:

   - **Discount:** Enable discount on products.
   - **Margins:** Show margins on orders.

3. Click **Save changes**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/set_manage_pricing.png" alt="Manage Pricing settings page with the Discount and Margins options" />

## Where discounts show

With **Discount** on, the **Order Line** table of a quotation can show a **Discount (%)** column. It is hidden by default. To show it:

1. Click the **column icon** at the right end of the table header.
2. Tick **Discount (%)**. You can tick **Margin** and **Margin (%)** here too.
3. Click **Apply columns**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/discount_colmgr_ticked.png" alt="Column list with Margin, Margin (%) and Discount (%) ticked and the Apply columns button" />

Then enter a percentage on each line. In this example, 5 Camping Hammocks at 65 with a **Discount (%)** of 10 give an **Amount** of 292.50.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/quotation_discount_column.png" alt="Order Line table with Margin, Margin (%) and Discount (%) columns and a 10 percent discount" />

## Where margins show

With **Margins** on, the **Order Line** table has two more columns, **Margin** and **Margin (%)**. Both are hidden by default. The order view also shows a **Margin** line under the totals, for example `$10,293.97 (544.7%)`.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/order_view_margin.png" alt="Order view totals with the Margin line under Amount Total" />

::: info
With a setting off, its columns do not show on the quotation.
:::
