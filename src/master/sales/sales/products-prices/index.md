# Products and prices

Products are the goods and services you sell. This group explains how to create products, how variants work, and how to control prices with price lists, discounts, margins and currencies.

> **In simple words:** Set up what you sell, and set the price you sell it at.

## In this group

- [Manage products](./manage-products.md): Create and maintain goods and services.
- [Product variants](./product-variants.md): Sell one product in several versions, such as sizes or colors.
- [Price lists](./price-lists.md): Sell at different prices to different customers.
- [Discounts and margins](./discounts-margins.md): Show discounts and margins on quotations.
- [Sell in other currencies](./sell-in-other-currencies.md): quote in another currency with a price list and an exchange rate.

## Turn features on first

Some features are off by default. Turn them on in `Sales → Settings → Manage Products` and `Sales → Settings → Manage Pricing`. See [Sales settings](../configuration/settings.md).
