# Sales

The **Sales** module takes a customer from first quote to final invoice. Use it to prepare quotations, confirm them as sales orders, manage your products and prices, and invoice what you sold.

Everything follows one flow: a **customer** receives a **quotation**, the quotation is **confirmed** as a sales order, and the order is **invoiced**.

> **In simple words:** Sales is where you quote, sell and invoice.

## How the module is organised

### Quotations and orders

Create quotes, send them and turn them into orders.

- [Quotations and orders](./quotations-orders/index.md): overview of the group.
- [Create a quotation](./quotations-orders/create-quotation.md): build a quote with order lines.
- [Optional products](./quotations-orders/optional-products.md): suggest extra items on a quote.
- [Send and confirm a quotation](./quotations-orders/send-and-confirm-quotation.md): email the quote and confirm it.
- [Manage orders](./quotations-orders/manage-orders.md): follow confirmed orders.
- [Manage customers](./quotations-orders/manage-customers.md): keep the people you sell to.

### Invoicing

Invoice the customer for what you sold.

- [Invoicing](./invoicing/index.md): overview of the group.
- [Invoicing policies](./invoicing/invoicing-policies.md): invoice ordered or delivered quantities.
- [Order to invoice](./invoicing/order-to-invoice.md): orders that are ready to invoice.
- [Order to upsell](./invoicing/order-to-upsell.md): orders where you can sell more.

### Products and prices

- [Products and prices](./products-prices/index.md): overview of the group.
- [Manage products](./products-prices/manage-products.md): create goods and services.

- [Discounts and margins](./products-prices/discounts-margins.md): discount lines and see your profit.
- [Sell in other currencies](./products-prices/sell-in-other-currencies.md): quote in another currency.

## Where to start

1. Review the [settings](./configuration/settings.md) and turn on the features you need.
2. Create your [products](./products-prices/manage-products.md) and a [customer](./quotations-orders/manage-customers.md).
3. [Create your first quotation](./quotations-orders/create-quotation.md), confirm it and invoice it.
