# Packagings

A packaging is a pack size for one product, such as a pack of 10. It has its own quantity and an optional barcode. Use packagings to sell or move products in fixed amounts.

> **In simple words:** A packaging says how many items sit in one pack.

::: info
Turn on **Packagings** in [Settings](./settings.md) to use this feature.
:::

## Overview

Go to `Sales → Configurations → Packagings → Products`.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/cfg_packagings_list.png" alt="Sales → Configurations → Packagings list with New Packaging button" />

The list shows **Name**, **Product**, **Qty**, **Barcode** and **Company**. Use **Group by** to group by **Product**, **Created At** or **Updated At**. You can filter by **Product**.

## Create a packaging

1. Go to `Sales → Configurations → Packagings → Products`.
2. Click **New Packaging**. A window opens.
3. Fill in the fields.

   - **_Name\*:_** Enter the pack name, such as Pack Of 10.
   - **_Barcode:_** Enter a barcode for the pack.
   - **_Product\*:_** Select the product.
   - **_Qty\*:_** Enter the number of items in one pack.
   - **_Company:_** Select the company. It defaults to your current company.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/product_packaging_form.png" alt="Packaging window with name, barcode, product, quantity and company" />

4. Click **Create**.

## Actions

- **View:** Open the packaging.
- **Edit:** Change its fields.
- **Delete:** Remove the packaging.
