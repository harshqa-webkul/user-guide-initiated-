# Settings

Settings turn **Sales** features on or off for the whole company. They are split into four pages. Each page has its own **Save changes** button.

> **In simple words:** Switch on only what your sales process needs.

## Manage Products

Go to `Sales → Settings → Manage Products`.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/set_manage_products.png" alt="Sales → Settings → Manage Products page showing the four toggles and Save changes" />

- **_Variants:_** Allow products to have multiple variants. Turn it on to sell one product in versions such as size or colour. See [Product variants](../products-prices/product-variants.md).
- **_Unit of Measure:_** Allow products to have a unit of measure. Turn it on to sell in units such as dozens. See [UOM categories](./uom-categories.md).
- **_Packagings:_** Allow products to have multiple packagings. Turn it on to define pack sizes. See [Packagings](./packagings.md).
- **_Price Lists:_** Allow products to be sold at prices set by a price list. See [Price lists](../products-prices/price-lists.md).

Click **Save changes** to apply.

## Manage Invoice

Go to `Sales → Settings → Manage Invoice`.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/set_manage_invoice.png" alt="Sales → Settings → Manage Invoice page showing Invoice Policy options" />

- **_Invoice Policy:_** Define how invoices are generated from sales orders. Choose one option.
  - **Ordered Quantities:** Invoice what the customer ordered.
  - **Delivered Quantities:** Invoice what you delivered.

Click **Save changes** to apply. Read more in [Invoicing policies](../invoicing/invoicing-policies.md).

## Manage Pricing

Go to `Sales → Settings → Manage Pricing`.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/set_manage_pricing.png" alt="Sales → Settings → Manage Pricing page showing Discount and Margins toggles" />

- **_Discount:_** Enable discount on products. Turn it on to give a discount on order lines.
- **_Margins:_** Show margins on orders. Turn it on to see your profit while you work on an order.

Click **Save changes** to apply. Read more in [Discounts and margins](../products-prices/discounts-margins.md).

## Manage Quotation & Order

Go to `Sales → Settings → Manage Quotation & Order`.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/set_manage_quotation.png" alt="Sales → Settings → Manage Quotation & Order page showing validity days and Lock Confirm Sales" />

- **_Default Quotation Validity\*:_** Enter the default number of days a quotation is valid. The field is required and the value is in days.
- **_Lock Confirm Sales:_** Turn on to lock the sales order after confirmation.

Click **Save changes** to apply. Read more in [Send and confirm a quotation](../quotations-orders/send-and-confirm-quotation.md).

::: tip
Turn on **Variants** before you create product attributes, so the options are ready when you build a product.
:::
