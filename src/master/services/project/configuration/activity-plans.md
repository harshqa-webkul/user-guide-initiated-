# Activity plans

An activity plan is a saved set of follow-up steps for a project or task. Each step is an activity, such as a call or an email, with an owner and a timing. When you start the plan, all its activities are scheduled in one go.

> **In simple words:** An activity plan is a checklist of scheduled follow-ups that you launch from Chatter with one click.

Open `Project → Configurations → Activity Plans`.

## Overview

The list shows **Name**, a **Status** icon, **Created At** and **Updated At**. Use the row menu for **Edit**, **Delete**, **Restore** and **Force delete**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/project/cf_activity_plans_list.png" alt="Activity Plans list with the Status column" />

## Create an activity plan

The example creates a plan named **Launch follow-up**.

1. Click **New Activity Plan**. The **Create Activity Plan** window opens.
2. Fill in the form.

   - **_Name:_** (required) A clear name, for example **Launch follow-up**.
   - **_Status:_** A toggle. It is on by default. Turn it off to stop the plan from being offered.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/project/cf_activity_plan_form.png" alt="Activity plan form with name Launch follow-up and Status on" />

3. Click **Create**. The plan is saved and shows in the list. Click **Edit** on its row to add steps.

## Add activity templates

Each step of the plan is an activity template. On the **Edit Activity Plan** page, find the **Activity Plan Templates** section and click **New activity plan template**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/project/cf_activity_templates_tab.png" alt="Activity Plan Templates section of the plan Launch follow-up with one step listed" />

Fill in the sections of the form.

### Activity Details

- **_Activity Type:_** (required) The kind of activity, such as a call or an email.
- **_Summary:_** A short title for the step.
- **_Note:_** More detail for the person who does it.

### Assignment

- **_Assignment:_** (required) Who owns the step. Options are **Ask at launch**, **Default user**, **Coach**, **Manager** and **Employee**.
- **_Assignee:_** Shows only for **Default user**. Pick the user.

### Delay Information

- **_Delay Count:_** How many units to wait.
- **_Delay Unit:_** **Minutes**, **Hours**, **Days** or **Weeks**.
- **_Delay From:_** **Before Plan Date** or **After Plan Date**. This says what the delay counts from.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/project/cf_activity_template_form.png" alt="Create Activity Plan Template window with activity type To-Do, summary Send launch email and a delay of 2 days" />

Click **Create** to save the step. Add more steps the same way.

## Use a plan

1. Open a project or a task.
2. Click **Chatter**, then use the activity plan action.
3. Pick the plan. Its activities are scheduled for you.

Only plans whose **Status** is on can be picked. See [Chatter](../../../getting-started/chatter.md) and [Activities](../../../getting-started/activities.md).

::: tip
Use **Ask at launch** when the owner changes each time. You then choose the person when you start the plan.
:::
